# STORYBOARD — Koi Zauber (swaibian.com promo)

## The decisions

- **Message:** No magic. Just work. Swaibian builds AI products that actually work — and this ad is not another one of *those* ads.
- **Audience and arc:** prospects and partners meeting Swaibian for the first time. Arc: play the median AI ad → kill it on screen → state the stance → prove it with six real products, one at a time → close on the brand line.
- **Format:** 1920×1080, ~60s, **no voiceover**, music-driven, English on-screen copy only. No captions needed (no speech). Durations are provisional until the music track lands; cuts will be quantized to its grid.
- **The spine:** *the switch.* One click kills the fake world mid-glow; from that instant every claim arrives on real captured material. Callback: a small "KOI ZAUBER" stamp presses onto the end card — the film's own maker's mark, answering the kill.
- **Brand, from the capture** (frame.md holds the normative values): warm paper `#FAF8F5`; petrol `#0B4A6F` as the only accent; near-black `#171717` headlines; muted `#656D72` body; petrol tints at 4/8/15/20%; radii 10–14px cards and 100px pills; hairlines 1–1.5px; **no shadows**. Type: Neue Haas Grotesk Text Pro (400/700 + italics), staged locally. The brand's mint (`#92EAD4` family) appears only inside captured screens and as the one petrol→mint gradient on the closing "AI, Made Simple." — a faithful lift of the site's own hero headline.
- **Off-system exception:** Frames 01–02 deliberately leave `frame.md` — the spoof act is generic AI-ad neon, on purpose, and dies on screen. Everything from the kill onward obeys the spec.
- **Bans (this film):** no glow or particles outside the spoof act; **no fake product UI** — every product screen is a real capture; no stock robots or circuit-board imagery; no slideshow rhythm (each proof frame develops, it is not a fresh card); no screensaver motion (nothing moves that says nothing); no second accent hue beyond the blessed mint gradient; no "seamless"/"unlock the power of" copy.
- **Held frame:** the black beat right after the click (F2) — nothing moves while "No magic." lands. A second hold closes the film on the wordmark.
- **Truthfulness:** all six product screens are real captures from www.swaibian.com; product lines compress the site's own copy, no numbers or features are invented. The opening spoof is explicitly fiction.
