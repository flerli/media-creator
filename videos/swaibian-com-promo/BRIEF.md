## Intent

A 60-second promo for www.swaibian.com — Swaibian AI Solutions, Stuttgart.
"Swabian Heritage. Silicon Valley Innovation." Concept **Koi Zauber (No Magic)**:
open with the generic AI-promo cliché — neon brain, particles, swelling synth —
and switch it off mid-glow. The back half proves Swaibian with all six products
(BrainWavr, ScaiCo, Plaza, BookBloom, Notaria, Tendr) doing real, useful work on
captured site screens; close on "AI, Made Simple."

Voice: dry Swabian understatement with warmth — the About page's own humor
("not abstract AI demos", dialect winks) drives the tone. The user's ask was
"promo, be creative"; the concept was pitched and chosen, and every brief option
was accepted as recommended. No narration; music-driven — statement cards land
one beat at a time and real screens carry the proof.

## Customizations

- **Website capture enabled** (route default): crawl www.swaibian.com for screens,
  brand tokens, and assets; captured screens are the video's featured assets.
- **Device-framed proof:** the six product screens presented in clean, matching
  device frames — restyle ready-made mockup scenes to the captured palette.
- **Cut to the beat:** the proof tour's cuts and accents land on the music's
  beat grid.
