[p] A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING
[h2] You can start where you are.
[summary] Do I need to know how to code?
[p] No. You describe how the work should happen in your own words. swaibian one helps turn that knowledge into a Use Case you can review and use.
[summary] Who is the authoring team?
[p] The authoring team is the digital help inside swaibian one that works with you to create your Use Case. It asks questions and shapes your Expert Team, Workflow, and Dashboard with you.
[summary] Do I need to have the whole process figured out?
[p] Start with what you know: the outcome, the steps you usually take, and the checks that matter. The authoring team helps clarify the details. You review the Use Case before putting it to work.
[summary] What’s the difference between a Use Case and a Task?
[p] A Use Case is your reusable way of handling a type of work. A Task is one real case you run through it: one request, one bid, or one candidate. Each Task has its own Dashboard.
[summary] Can I see why a result needs my attention?
[p] You can follow the steps, review the checks and supporting evidence, and decide what to do next. You can ask for rework or take over when your judgement is needed.
[summary] What if the way we work changes?
[p] Describe the change in the conversation. Refine your Use Case, review the update, and reuse the knowledge you’ve already captured.
[summary] How do we keep getting better as we work?
[p] After a Task is completed, you can run a retrospective. The authoring team reviews how the case really went, together with you, and refines your Expert Team, Workflow, and Dashboard — an agile way of working, built into the process.
